How to play

1. Ask a question from today’s lesson. If the student answers correctly, click Correct answer → choose.

2. The student chooses Top, Middle or Bottom, then a numbered screen in that row.

3. Green arrow: they win! Q: ask another question and mark the answer correct or incorrect. Flashing hot spot: they lose this turn.

You decide the reward. Ask all questions aloud, using any topic or year group. Each screen independently has a 60% chance of a hot spot, 20% of a question and 20% of an instant win. Exact totals vary between boards.

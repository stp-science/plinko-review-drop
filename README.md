# Plinko Review Drop

A classroom review game inspired by Basketball Review Shootout. Ask a question aloud, let a student with a correct answer choose a numbered disc, then press Drop.

- Six reusable discs, animated peg board, twelve alternating WIN and LOSE slots.
- Each drop has ten fair random bounces followed by a weighted final bounce: 30% win and 70% lose, independently of previous drops. Disc choice and previous results do not affect the odds. Short sequences need not have equal wins and losses.
- The outcome follows the random bouncing route. All 10,240 equally likely combinations of early bounces and final draws have been checked: 3,072 win and 7,168 lose.
- Sound on by default, peg clicks, landing thump, distinct win and loss sounds, full screen, counters, reduced-motion support, keyboard controls and responsive layout.
- No libraries, accounts, tracking or student data storage.
